Otrium: Platform & Conversion Optimization

Reducing friction across the Otrium shopping journey through rigorous A/B testing, checkout optimization, and scalable design systems—driving €1.3M in annualized GMV impact.

Company

Otrium

Role

Product Designer

Focus

A/B Testing · Checkout UX · UI Architecture

Impact

+28.4% total conversion uplift

The challenge

Fixing product-discovery friction with data, while supporting higher conversion.

My scope at Otrium was broad but deeply metrics-driven: "How can UX improvements across the shopping journey help users understand, discover, and use key features more easily?"

Instead of focusing on a single feature, I tackled two major design tracks alongside proactive platform work. The first was optimizing Store Credit into a trustworthy, frictionless retention feature. The second was diagnosing and fixing major product-discovery friction on the homepage and platform using rigorous A/B testing.

A/B Testing Store Credit UI Systems Business Impact
01

Discovery

Turning the "escape room" into a clear path

Initial research revealed that users were treating the homepage like an "escape room," frantically scanning for the fastest way out to the search bar. To fix this, I initiated a series of platform-wide A/B tests using Coframe.

Through testing, I learned that hierarchy and placement are the biggest homepage levers. By moving high-intent content above interstitials, we achieved a massive +14.9% conversion win on homepage hierarchy alone.

"A failed test isn't a failed idea, it's a diagnosis."

  • Diagnosing Data: An initial USP block test underperformed. Instead of scrapping it, data revealed a targeting error. We fixed the segment, re-ran the test, and secured a +10% conversion win.
  • Balancing Needs: Marketing wants more banners; UX wants clarity. I designed a desktop user-controlled carousel and a mobile swipe layout with clear indicators to satisfy both.
  • Mobile Discoverability: Proved that a compact, fully visible grid beats a carousel on mobile, driving an additional +2.0% lift.
02

Store Credit

O-Credit

Store Credit is a vital retention tool, but users were confused by abstract points systems. Through prototyping and user interviews, I mapped the integration of a "My Vouchers" screen directly into the cart and checkout flows.

The ultimate pivot was scrapping gamification entirely in favor of a brutalist, clear €10 toggle within the checkout (evolving from Version 1 abstract points directly to the Final Version 7 clear toggle). By ensuring the credit functioned visually as a simple monetary discount, user confidence skyrocketed.

Edge Cases

Every state was designed upfront—including empty states and technical errors—so exactly what we tested was built.

Live Handoff

Maintained clean, annotated Figma files for live alignment with B2C engineers.

+150% Lift

The clear toggle interaction generated a +150% conversion lift in a rolling A/B/C/D test.

03

UI Systems

Scaling quality and protecting against debt

Beyond immediate feature work, I proactively audited the foundations of Otrium's UI framework. I reviewed and updated the core Ozone tokens under the hood, focusing on corner radius, blurs, and elevation.

Cleaner tokens mean a single source of truth and a vastly smoother developer handoff. Because as I learned: "A fix made in the system is a fix made everywhere."

  • Standardized the system so one styling change trickles down to every button, card, and modal.
  • Designed the platform's Megamenu from scratch (from wireframes to final UI) to improve global navigation.
  • Redesigned outdated iOS App Store imagery to ensure core brand themes carried from the store listing straight into the app.

Business Impact

Understanding first, conversion follows.

By diagnosing user friction through data and executing scalable design solutions, this body of work drove a total conversion uplift of +28.4% and an annualized GMV impact of €1.3M.

More importantly, this experience marked a profound personal shift: transforming from a junior dealing with impostor syndrome into an independent product builder who scopes, decides, and ships.
