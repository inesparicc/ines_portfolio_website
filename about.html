About me

I’m Ines, a Product Designer with a background in ICT & Media Design. I am incredibly passionate about the sweet spot where creative visual design meets human psychology—building digital experiences that not only look beautiful but actually solve real user problems.

I bring hands-on industry experience in e-commerce and product UX. Most recently, I shaped the Store Credit checkout experience at Otrium, where I learned how to balance complex business goals with crystal-clear user journeys through rigorous user testing, prototyping, and cross-functional collaboration.

Whether I am optimizing a high-traffic checkout flow, designing inclusive navigation concepts, or building physical furniture in my studio, I thrive on turning messy, complex challenges into structured and practical design decisions.
