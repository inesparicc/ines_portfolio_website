About Me

I’m Ines, a UX & media design student in Eindhoven. I focus on creating digital experiences that are intuitive, human-centred and grounded in research.

Through my ICT & Media Design studies, I’ve developed strong skills in user research, interaction design, prototyping and translating insights into clear product decisions. Alongside this, I’ve built technical understanding through front-end basics, APIs and working closely with developers.

I’ve designed for real companies through school projects and my internship, giving me experience with user needs, business goals and designing within real-world constraints.

This semester I’m exploring immersive technologies and working on a creative research project for an external partner - expanding my skillset while staying rooted in my main strength: human-centred UX.
