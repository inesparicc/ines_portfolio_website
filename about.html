About me

I’m Ines, a Product Designer based in Ireland. With a BSc in Information and Communication Technology, I am deeply passionate about the intersection of human psychology and functional design.

My design process is firmly rooted in user research. I specialize in translating complex behaviors into clear, intuitive digital products. Whether I am running usability tests, analyzing heatmaps, or structuring A/B experiments, I believe the best design decisions are driven by real human data, not just aesthetics.

Before focusing fully on digital product design, I built a foundation in physical crafting—from furniture making to sculpting. Working with physical materials taught me how to think critically about structure, form, and how people physically interact with the things they use. Today, I apply that same hands-on, structural thinking to building digital interfaces.
